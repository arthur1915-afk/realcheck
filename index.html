HOW IT WORKS

Trust is a two-way street.

REALCHECK is designed to make verification clear, mutual and respectful.

01
◉

Verify your identity

Use a specialist verification provider to check your identity. REALCHECK should receive a verification result—not store raw identity documents itself.

Both people receive the same random prompt and submit a fresh response within the time limit.

BUILT WITH CARE

Your identity is yours.
Keep it that way.

Verification should increase confidence without exposing sensitive personal information. The product will be designed around data minimisation, secure providers and clear user control.

Our principles ↗
⌑
Private by default

Government ID images should be handled by a specialist provider, not displayed on public profiles.

⇄
Mutual participation

Connection features should respect both people's choices and consent.

⛨
Safety controls

Blocking, reporting and account controls are core features—not afterthoughts.
